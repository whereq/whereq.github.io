whereq.com is open source — the map, the crawler, and everything it reads

2026年11月12日 · 阅读需 8 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

A pin is a very small sentence​

Stand on a street in Toronto and look at the address in front of you. A map gives you one claim about it: there is a pin here. A coffee shop. A hospital. A school. Maybe a parking garage.

But the place itself is a pile of claims, and the pin is the least interesting one of them.

Which school catchment is that address in? Can I walk to a library, and which one? Is the fuel station on the corner charging 1.42or1.42 or 1.42or1.79? Is that building inside a ward, or inside a neighbourhood, or in a ward and a neighbourhood? Did anything happen on this street last week?
