WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.11：鼠标有了右键，而且活过了存档

2026年11月17日 · 阅读需 6 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

按不出来的那个键​

你在游戏里点开一个菜单，右边一排选项，最右边那个是退出——在大多数 DOS 游戏里，退出就是鼠标右键：取消、返回。

DOS 鼠标有两个键，手机只有一根手指，右键根本不存在。于是游戏本身是完整的，而出口在你够不着的地方。

1.0.0.10 以及之前的每一个版本都是这样。

The button you could not press​

You are inside a game. You open a menu, and at the right end of the row of choices there is the way out — in most DOS games that way out is the right mouse button. Cancel. Back.

A DOS mouse had two buttons. Your phone has one finger, and no right button at all. So the game was complete and its exit was somewhere you could not reach.

That was 1.0.0.10, and every release before it.

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.12：存档搬出了应用

2026年11月17日 · 阅读需 9 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

「全没了」​

事情往往是这样发生的：手机存储满了，或者应用开始出怪问题，或者你只是想重装一遍。

你重新装上 APK，游戏压缩包还在你选的那个文件夹里，你按下 Play——游戏却从第一关开始了。

十个小时的进度没了。不是因为游戏丢了，而是因为应用没了。

在 1.0.0.12 之前，WhereQ-DOSBox 的情况就是这样。一个存档——整台机器、声音芯片、DOS 文件句柄——都躺在应用自己的私有目录里，而 Android 会在你卸载的那一刻把这个目录一起删掉。于是这个模拟器存在的意义——把一台跑着的 DOS 机器冻住、以后再接着跑——恰恰成了唯一保不住的那一样。

1.0.0.12 改了这件事。它还让读档之后靠鼠标操作的游戏不再卡死，也让模拟器读自己的版本号，而不是一个写死的旧数字。三件事，第一件最值得往下看。

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.12: your saves move out of the app

2026年11月17日 · 阅读需 9 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

"Everything is gone"​

It happens the boring way. The phone is full, or the app starts misbehaving, or you simply decide to reinstall it. You install the APK again, the game bundle is still in the folder you picked, you press Play — and the game starts at the very beginning.

Ten hours of progress, gone. Not because the game was lost. Because the app was.

That was the truth about WhereQ-DOSBox until this release. A save state — the frozen machine, the sound chips, the DOS file handles — lived inside the app's own private storage, and Android deletes that storage the moment the app is uninstalled. So the one thing the emulator exists to protect was the one thing that could not survive the app.

1.0.0.12 changes that. It also makes a restore leave a mouse-driven game alive instead of hung, and it makes the emulator read its own version instead of a stale literal. Three things, and the first one is the reason to read the rest.

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.8: save the whole machine, and a Resume button that resumes

2026年11月16日 · 阅读需 8 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

The button that lied​

Two releases ago I gave the launcher a Resume button. It looked like a promise: press it, and you are back in the game you were playing.

It was not. It started the last game from the beginning, every time.

I did not notice it myself, because on my desk the emulator process was still alive — the game had never really stopped, so there was nothing to resume. Then somebody on a phone pressed the button, lost an hour of progress, and told me plainly: the Resume button restarts the game.

They were right, and the fix was not a one-liner. It took a whole-machine snapshot.

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.9: the keyboard becomes a handheld console

2026年11月16日 · 阅读需 9 分钟
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

A compromise, wearing the shape of a keyboard​

The DOS picture is 4:3. A phone in landscape is 20:9. The difference is two black bars, one on each side of the picture, and for two releases those bars were where the keys lived.

They were not bad bars. The arrows went into the inverted T of a real keyboard, the modifiers and the editing keys into the other side, and the picture kept the whole middle of the screen. It worked.

It was also the wrong shape for the thing it was standing in for. A DOS game is played with the left thumb on the arrows and the right thumb on the keys, and every one of those keys was a long way from where a thumb expects it.
