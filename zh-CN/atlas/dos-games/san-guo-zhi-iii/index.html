What it is

三国志III — Romance of the Three Kingdoms III, in its Simplified Chinese DOS release. It is a grand strategy game: a map of the whole of China, the cities on it, the officers who hold them, and the markets they trade in. The archive calls it 三国志III, and this copy is the Chinese one — every word on the screen is 简体.

The bundle names its own maker. FMDRV.COM — the FM sound driver the launcher loads — carries the line "Copyright (C) 1991 KOEI CO.,LTD. JAPAN", and the game's own DOS shell is KOEI.COM. That shell is written into the bundle's SHELL.SYS, the file DOS reads at boot to decide what the command prompt is.

The copy is dated 1993: 23 of its 35 files carry that year, and the game's own program, MAIN.EXE, is dated 3 January 1994. The Chinese text is all there — the menus, the scenario list, the officers' names and the orders you give them are in 简体, not in a Japanese or English release.

The whole game is one directory of plain files: three executables, two COM loaders, the Chinese 16-dot fonts, six scenario files and a stack of .DAT data files. Nothing is compressed and nothing needs installing.

Set it up in WhereQ-DOSBox

There is one thing to change, and the app will not do it for you. Import the zip, open the game's row and tap Launch… — the command the app would run by itself is MAIN.EXE, and MAIN.EXE on its own exits immediately and drops you back at the DOS prompt. The launcher is KOEI.COM.

  • Import the zip into WhereQ-DOSBox — open it from the Files app on your phone and choose WhereQ-DOSBox, or use the app's own import.
  • Open the game's row and tap Launch….
  • Tap Clear, then tap the KOEI.COM chip. The script line reads KOEI, and that is right — DOS adds the .COM itself.
  • Tap Play. The app writes a RUN.BAT in the game folder and runs KOEI from it.

What KOEI.COM does, in order: it loads FMDRV.COM as the resident sound driver, then runs OPEN.EXE, then MAIN.EXE, and END.EXE when the game ends. The names are all in the launcher's own strings, in that order.

There is no installer to run and nothing to configure. Do not run OPEN.EXE or END.EXE yourself — KOEI.COM runs them for you, in the right order.

How to play

The opening is a run of cards. Each one is a dithered picture — a warrior's head in blue, a city gate — with a poem written down it in vertical columns. The first is 滾滾長江東逝水，浪花淘盡英雄, the poem that opens the Romance; the later ones tell the Yellow Turban rising — 十常侍弄權柄，陷害忠良亂朝廷。中平元年關癘氣，大賢良師他自稱。張角舉事造黃旗. Space moves the cards along.

The cards are followed by the password screen, and then the dragon and the tiger, and a card that says 「使用滑鼠時，請按滑鼠鍵. 使用鍵盤時，請接著鍵入.」 — use the mouse, or press a key to type. Press Enter and the title menu comes up: 1. 開始新遊戲, 2. 從進度檔案載入進度, 3. 登錄新武將.

Every menu in this game is a numbered one. Type the number and press Enter. A new game asks, in order: how many people are playing (0 to 8), which of the six scenarios, which lord you are, how hard (初級 or 高級), whether you want to watch the other countries' wars first, and whether it is 史實型 (history happens) or 假想型 (history does not). It then shows the whole setup back to you and asks 全部都可以了嗎(Y/N)?.

The main screen is a map of China. Across the top is the command row — 0.休養 1.軍事 2.人事 3.外交 4.情報 5.開發 6.謀略 7.商人 8.特別 9.功能 — and the bottom line asks you what you want to do: 『劉備，請向23.新野下命令(0-9)?』. The panel on the right is the city you are standing in: its population, its gold, its grain, its soldiers, its officers and its mood, with the market prices beside it and your ruler's portrait below.

Under 軍事 there are nine more: 移動, 輸送, 戰爭準備, 戰爭, 徵兵, 募兵, 訓練, 再編成 and 造船. Taking 徵兵 asks 派誰徵兵(0-13)? and opens a roster of your officers with their 武力; type the number of the officer you want, press Enter, and the game asks 徵兵幾百人(1-38)?. The men are taken, the city's soldiers go up, and the leftover is offered to another general.

One wrinkle worth knowing: when a roster of names is on screen, typing the number does not select the officer straight away — it moves the marker to that entry and marks it with a star. Press Enter as well, and the game takes it.

Verified on the emulator: the game starts from the KOEI.COM launcher, the opening cards and the dragon and tiger play, the title menu opens, a new game is set up from scratch, the map of China comes up, the 軍事 menu opens, and 徵兵 was carried through to the end — 100 soldiers recruited and the city's numbers changed. A battle was not fought, and the password screen was passed with the word "abc" rather than the one the game shows.

The password screen

After the opening cards, before the dragon and the tiger, the game asks 密碼: and, under it, 請輸入正確密碼 with the answer in red brackets — [孫李李]. That is the copy-protection screen, and in this release it does not check: the word "abc" was typed, Enter pressed, and the game carried on as if it had been right.

So you do not have to type 孫李李 — and you cannot easily, since the on-screen keyboard has no Chinese characters on it. Type anything and press Enter.
