What it is

三国志英杰传 — Romance of the Three Kingdoms: Heroic Legend — is KOEI's 1995 strategy role-playing game. You take the role of Liu Bei, and the campaign runs from the coalition that marches on Dong Zhuo through the long fight against Cao Wei. Officers join you as the story goes on, they level up, and what you equip them with decides how a battle ends.

This bundle is the Traditional Chinese DOS release, not the Japanese original: the text inside it is Big5, and the publisher of the Chinese release was 第三波 (Soft-World). The screen is VGA text mode with an animated status bar, and the game speaks in the voice of the 1990s Chinese translation — 開始新遊戲 for a new game, 讀取存檔 to load one, 功能設定 for the options, 觀看劇情簡介 to watch the story prologue.

The copyright line on the title screen reads © 1995 KOEI CO.,LTD, and the graphics credit inside the executable reads "TF-SSC … Programmed by T.FUJINUMA (TF-928) 1994,1995". The archive's installer string dates the release to 17 February 1995.

Set it up in WhereQ-DOSBox

This is the part that catches people out. Pressing Play on this bundle does not start the game — it drops you at a DOS prompt and nothing else happens. The game is not broken; it is waiting for the right launcher.

  • Import the zip into WhereQ-DOSBox — open it from the Files app on your phone and choose WhereQ-DOSBox, or use the app's own import.
  • Open the game's row and tap Launch… to open the launch script.
  • Tap Clear to empty the script field.
  • Tap the REKO3.COM chip to put REKO3 in the script field.
  • Tap Play.

REKO3.COM is the game's own launcher, and it is the only thing that starts the game correctly. It loads the sound driver (SBOPL2.COM), the graphics driver (GRPDRV.EXE) and the font driver (TFDED.COM), and then runs OPEN.EXE for the intro, MAIN.EXE for the game itself and END.EXE for the ending.

It also needs the game on two drives. WhereQ-DOSBox already mounts A: and B: to the game folder, which is what REKO3 expects — the game reads A:BAKDATA.R3 and B:IPPAN0.R3 — so there is nothing to mount by hand.

Why Play on its own fails: the app picks the biggest executable that is not an installer or a driver, and in this bundle that is MAIN.EXE. MAIN.EXE is the game without its drivers and without the launcher, so it starts, cannot draw, and falls back to the DOS prompt. Run INSTALL.EXE and you make it worse — the archive is already the installed layout.

How to play

The first thing you see is a password prompt: 密碼, with the line 請輸入正確密碼 [木土日] underneath. Type anything at all and press Enter — the gate takes whatever you type and lets you through into the menu. It is a copy-protection gate, not a secret; the 木土日 hint belongs to something else inside the game.

The menu has four items: 開始新遊戲 (new game), 讀取存檔 (load a save), 功能設定 (options) and 觀看劇情簡介 (watch the story prologue). They are mouse-driven — the keyboard will not move that cursor.

  • Give DOSBox the mouse: press RMB on the on-screen keyboard (or the right mouse button on a Bluetooth keyboard) to switch mouse capture on, then tap the screen to click.
  • Tap 開始新遊戲 to start a new campaign.
  • Press Enter on the KOEI logo screen to move into the story prologue, then keep pressing Enter to step through it.
  • From there the campaign proper begins: the map, the officers, the battles.

Two things worth knowing before you start. The campaign is decided in the planning stages — which officers you bring, which treasure and which horse each of them carries, and how you spend your turns — so the map matters more than the fighting does. And if you want to see how far Liu Bei can go, the strategy guides for this game describe a way to raise him straight to level 99: in a non-battle state, move the arrow onto Liu Bei's portrait and hold the left mouse button, then answer 是 when the question comes up, and do the same with the bead at the top left.
