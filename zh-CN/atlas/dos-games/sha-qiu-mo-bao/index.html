What it is

沙丘魔堡 is the Chinese release of Dune II — the 1992 real-time strategy game from Cryo Interactive and Virgin Games. The Chinese title is 第三波's own: the intro screen reads VIRGIN GAMES under a Chinese line that says 第三波文化事業 1992 強強滾推出, and the whole game is in Traditional Chinese.

The archive carries the original installer, and it names the authors: INSTALL.EXE holds the line "(c) 1992 Cryo & Virgin Games". That is the game as it shipped — on floppies, with the first disk called out in the installer's own text, "INSERT DUNE DISK 1 IN DRIVE A:".

The Chinese release is a translation of the whole thing, not just the interface: the introduction is narrated in Chinese ("在未來的世界裡 人類已發現了無數的星系…"), the map is called 沙丘地圖, and the build panel speaks in the language of the 1990s Chinese localisation.

Set it up in WhereQ-DOSBox

This one needs no setup at all. The bundle has exactly one batch file, DUNE.BAT, and the app picks it on its own — so import the zip and press Play.

  • Import the zip into WhereQ-DOSBox — open it from the Files app on your phone and choose WhereQ-DOSBox, or use the app's own import.
  • Open the game's row and tap Play. The app starts DUNE.BAT.
  • DUNE.BAT turns the sound off, plays the intro logo and starts the game with the switches the release was made for.

The whole of DUNE.BAT is three lines: @echo off, logo, and then duneprg CHIVGA SDB22227 EMS 386. The first runs LOGO.EXE, which is the Virgin and 第三波 title card. The second starts DUNEPRG.EXE with the language and display switches for the Chinese text on VGA, a Sound Blaster setting, and expanded memory on a 386.

Do not run INSTALL.EXE. It is the 1992 floppy installer: it wants DUNE DISK 1 in drive A:, asks for 2 500 KB of free space, and copies the game to C:\DUNE. The archive is already the installed layout — the installer would only put the game in the wrong place.

How to play

The intro plays first: the Virgin Games logo, then the Chinese line naming 第三波, and then a narrated introduction over a desert scene. Press Space on the on-screen keyboard to step through the narration — one press, one line.

Then the house-selection screen comes up: a throne room, the name of a house on the panel at the bottom, and the mouse cursor. This is the one screen where the mouse matters, and the app needs a little care with it.

  • Give DOSBox the mouse first: press RMB on the on-screen keyboard (or the right mouse button on a Bluetooth keyboard) to switch mouse capture on.
  • Move the cursor with the arrow keys on the on-screen keyboard. The cursor is the arrow on the picture; the arrow keys move it.
  • A quick tap only moves the cursor — it does not click. Hold the press for about half a second before letting go, and the click lands.
  • Click the house crest at the top-left or the top-right of the screen to take that house.
  • Click the player number in the panel at the bottom — 1 or 2 — to confirm, and the world map comes up.

The world map is 沙丘地圖, and the panel below it reads 結束地圖模式 — that is the hint for the control pad on the right, and pressing it takes you back out of the map. The panel carries the numbers of the houses in play, so a second player can take the other house on the same screen.

Verified on the emulator: the intro plays, the house is taken, and the world map opens with the base on it and the map panel showing the troop count. The build phase was not played through — that is the next thing to work out, and the comments on this page are where it should go.
