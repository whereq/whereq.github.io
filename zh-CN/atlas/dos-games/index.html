What this shelf is

A shelf of DOS games, each one documented the way a person who has just installed it would want it read: here is the bundle, here is exactly what you have to do to start it, and here is what every key does.

These are the games that run on WhereQ-DOSBox — the DOS emulator built natively for Android, one page away in the Universe. They were tested on an emulator running the app, and the steps on each page are the steps that actually worked, including the ones that turned out to be wrong on the first attempt.

This shelf holds the games that have been played through on an emulator. Every other game in the archive has a page too, in the catalogue at /dos-games/ — the facts read out of the bundle itself, with a download and the setup its shape implies. Those pages say plainly that nobody has played them yet. The shape of every page stays the same as the shelf grows.

How to read a game page

Each page runs in the same order: what the game is, the download with its checksum, the setup in WhereQ-DOSBox, how to play it, what is inside the bundle, and where every fact came from. The download comes first on purpose — the page is worth little if the reader has to hunt for the file.

Every page also carries a notice about the download, and every page carries a comment thread. If a step does not work on your device, or you know a shorter way in, say so in the comments — that is what the thread is for.

The whole archive

About the downloads

The archives on these pages are published for study, communication and preservation only. They are the work of their authors and publishers, who hold the rights in them; publishing one here claims none of those rights and waives none of them.

If you believe a download here breaks a licence, a copyright or any other right, tell us and we will take it down. The same goes for a bundle that is missing something — say so in the comments and it will be replaced.

真人快打The Fighting Game

The 1993 DOS conversion of Midway's arcade fighter, published by Acclaim and converted by Probe. It imports, Play starts it, and then it sits there looking dead — because the copy-protection screen wants a word and the title screen wants F1. Both are written down.

S — names that start with “S”3 pages

The games on this shelf whose name begins with S.

三国志英杰传The Strategy RPG

The 1995 KOEI strategy RPG in its Traditional Chinese DOS release. You play Liu Bei, gathering officers and holding the line against Dong Zhuo and the Cao Wei campaign that follows. It is the one game on this shelf that refuses to start when you press Play — it needs a different launcher, and the page says which one.

1995Download the bundle
沙丘魔堡The Real-Time Strategy

The 1992 Cryo and Virgin Games real-time strategy game, in the Chinese release published by 第三波. Import the zip, press Play, and the batch file runs the intro and the game for you. The one thing the page has to explain is the house-selection screen: it is mouse-driven, and a quick tap moves the cursor without clicking.

1992Download the bundle
三国志IIIThe Grand Strategy

The third of KOEI's Three Kingdoms games, in its Simplified Chinese DOS release. Import the zip, then change one line: the app starts MAIN.EXE on its own and the game exits at once — the launcher is KOEI.COM. Once that is set, the whole game opens: the opening cards with their poems, the dragon and the tiger, six scenarios from 189 to 235, and a map of China you run a war from.

1993Download the bundle

炎龙骑士团II‧黄金城之谜The Fantasy Strategy

The 1995 sequel to 炎龙骑士团, in its Traditional Chinese DOS release. Import the zip and press Play: the app picks FD2.EXE and the game runs, intro animation and all. The page writes down the route in — which key skips the film, how the story dialogue advances, how you move on the field map, and how a battle is fought down to the damage and the experience points.
