What it is

whereq.ca is the research station. It is where the universe's work happens before it ships: prototypes, experiments, the shared component library, and the map that shows how every application fits together.

It is also the front door. Someone who wants to use the universe does not need thirteen URLs — whereq.ca is the one place that lists them, and it is where a new application is announced before it graduates.

How it fits the universe

whereq.ca answers "why" — why a thing exists and whether it should. It is the only application in the universe whose output is not a product but a decision: graduate, iterate, or drop.
