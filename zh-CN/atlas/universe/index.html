One cloud, thirteen worlds

The WhereQ Universe is a set of applications that share a single spine. whereq.cloud owns the schema, runs the collectors that fill it, and publishes it through one versioned API. The applications above it read from that contract instead of building their own.

That is the whole idea. A market-news desk, a freight platform, a tutoring companion and a photo vault are not four unrelated products that happen to share a name — they are four readers of the same database, each one shaped around a different question a person actually asks.

How the universe fits together

It divides into three layers.

  • The foundation — the data platform (whereq.cloud), the identity layer (keytomarvel.com), the research station (whereq.ca), and this site, the universe's public voice.
  • The engines — the spatial engine (whereq.com) and the temporal engine (chroniq.cc), which own geography and time respectively.
  • The products — flowdesk.top, caijing.today, qhaul.ca, catobigato.com, whereq.cc, whereq-dosbox and ccbus.cc: each one a finished thing a person can open and use.

The layering is what keeps the universe honest. A product can change its interface without anyone touching the data, and the data can be corrected once, everywhere, without shipping six patches.

How to read this atlas

Every application has one page here, and every page has the same shape: what it is, what it does, how it fits the universe, and what is under the hood. The pages are written to be read in any order.

Start with the six questions below, then use the tree on the left to jump to any application. Each page opens with a summary card — its role, its status, what it can do, and where to go next — so you can tell in ten seconds whether it is the one you are looking for.

The six questions

Every application in the universe answers one of six questions. The ones below are the owners — the application whose whole job is to answer that question for everything else.

  • WHEREMaps, routing, places and neighbourhood boundaries — where things are.whereq.com
  • WHOOne passport, one identity, every application in the universe.keytomarvel.com
  • WHENThe real calendar — holidays, lunar dates, weather and availability.chroniq.cc
  • WHYWhere an idea is tested before it is allowed to graduate.whereq.ca
  • WHATWhat the universe has shipped, written up and published.whereq.github.io
  • HOWOne centralized data cloud that every application reads from.whereq.cloud
