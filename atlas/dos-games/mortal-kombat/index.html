What it is

真人快打 is the DOS version of Mortal Kombat — the Midway arcade fighter that arrived on the PC in 1993 as a DOS/4GW program. The credit inside MK.EXE reads "THE PROBE CONVERSION TEAM:", so Probe Software did the conversion, and Acclaim published it. The strings and the graphics files inside the archive name both: ACCLAIM.GRA and PROBE.GRA.

The dates in the archive agree with that. The data files run from May 1993 to December 1993, and the game itself carries the line "ALLOW 4-6 WEEKS FOR DELIVERY — OFFER EXPIRES NOVEMBER 1, 1993".

The roster is the arcade one, with two extra: Kano, Johnny Cage, Rayden, Liu Kang, Scorpion, Sub-Zero and Sonya, plus Goro and Shang Tsung. The fight modes are MIRROR MATCH and three levels of ENDURANCE.

Set it up in WhereQ-DOSBox

This one is the easy case: import the zip, and the app finds MK.EXE on its own and Play starts the game. There is no launcher to set, no driver to load first.

  • Import the zip into WhereQ-DOSBox — open it from the Files app on your phone and choose WhereQ-DOSBox, or use the app's own import.
  • Tap Play. The game starts.

One thing to leave alone: the bundle contains CRACK.COM, and you should not run it. It prints "File Was NOT Successfully Patched!" and changes nothing — the MK.EXE in this archive is already the finished WOLVERINE crack, so there is nothing left to patch. Running it only wastes a DOS prompt.

The bundle also ships the four sound drivers of the period — ADLIB.DRV, ROLAND.DRV, SBLASTER.DRV and BEEP.DRV. Which one the game picks is up to the sound card DOSBox presents, so if the music is thin, that is the setting to change rather than anything in the archive.

How to play

Here is the part that is not written down anywhere. The game starts, and it looks stuck.

  • First screen: a copy-protection prompt that asks for a word. Type any word and press Enter — it takes anything.
  • Then the title screen and the attract cycle. Press F1 to start the game.
  • F1 only works while the start screen is up. If a demonstration match is playing, keep pressing F1 — pressing it during a match starts story mode instead.
  • On the on-screen keyboard, F1 lives in the Fn layer. On a Bluetooth or USB keyboard, press F1 directly: WhereQ-DOSBox forwards hardware keys into DOSBox.

From F1 you get CHOOSE YOUR FIGHTER. The arrows move the cursor and a punch key — S by default — confirms the fighter you are pointing at. In the match itself the arrows move, the same keys punch and kick, and F10 opens the game's own setup.

That setup screen is worth knowing. CONTROL 1: KEYBOARD is where every action is bound: the game asks you to press the key for UP, for DOWN, for LEFT, for RIGHT, then HIGH PUNCH, LOW PUNCH, BLOCK, HIGH KICK and LOW KICK, and whatever you press is what it uses. Because WhereQ-DOSBox forwards a hardware keyboard into DOSBox, you can bind any key you like. The same screen sets the difficulty (VERY EASY to VERY HARD), the speed (LOW (386) or HIGH (486)), the sound card (NO SOUND, SPEAKER, ADLIB, ROLAND, SOUND BLASTER, or ROLAND & SBLASTER) and the control device.

Verified on the emulator: Sonya against Raiden and Sub-Zero against Kano both start, the fighter moves when the arrow is pressed, and the opponent's health bar goes down.
