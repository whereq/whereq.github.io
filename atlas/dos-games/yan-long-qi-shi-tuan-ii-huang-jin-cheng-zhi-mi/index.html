What it does
  • An opening chapter that plays as a film: a castle under the moon, the two kings on their thrones, and Saul before his father.
  • A field map you walk around a tile at a time, with characters who talk when you walk into them.
  • A tactical layer under the story: a command ring of four icons around the selected character — physical attack, spell attack, items, rest — that ends in a real battle. What it is

炎龙骑士团II‧黄金城之谜 — Fantasy War 2, in its Traditional Chinese DOS release. The title screen names the company that made it: 漢堂, under the English line DYNASTY INTERNATIONAL INFORMATION CO., LTD.

It is a 32-bit program. FD2.EXE is a DOS/4GW executable — Rational Systems' protected-mode run-time, copyright 1987 to 1993 — carrying a WATCOM C/C++ 32-bit run-time of its own. The bundle's own newest date is the audio library, Miles Design Audio Interface Library V3.02 of 18 January 1995, which is where the 1995 comes from.

The sound is set up already. DIG.INI and MDI.INI both point at a Creative Labs Sound Blaster 16 on port 220h — SB16.DIG and SBPRO2.MDI — so the game has sound without any setup of your own.

Set it up in WhereQ-DOSBox

There is nothing to set up. The bundle has one executable that matters, FD2.EXE, and the app finds it on its own — so import the zip and press Play.

  • Import the zip into WhereQ-DOSBox — open it from the Files app on your phone and choose WhereQ-DOSBox, or use the app's own import.
  • Open the game's row and tap Play. The app writes a RUN.BAT in the game folder and runs FD2 from it.
  • Wait. The first thing on the screen is the DOSBox banner, and then the game's own title animation.

Do not run SETSOUND.EXE unless the sound is wrong. It is the Miles configuration tool, and it writes DIG.INI and MDI.INI — which are already in the archive, already pointing at a Sound Blaster 16 on port 220h. The game reads them as they are.

How to play

The opening is a film, and it is a long one: a castle under the moon, a face made of red brush strokes, the 漢堂 card, and the silver 2 over the red wordmark. Press Space on the on-screen keyboard to skip through it.

After the film comes the title menu: START, LOAD and CONTINUE, in English, on a blue screen with a red banner. Space starts a new game — the mouse does not work on this screen, so use the keyboard.

Then the story plays as a dialogue, with a portrait on the right and the text on the left. The first line is 『兒臣索爾，晉見父王陛下』 — Saul before his father the king — and in the throne room a little later: 『到這裡來，讓义王好好看看你。這一陣子國務繁忙，我們父子兩個也很久沒聚聚了』. Space, Enter and Escape all move the dialogue along.

After the story you are on the field: a map of grass and paths, with your character standing on it and other people around. The arrow keys move. Walk into someone and the game starts talking to them — the first character you meet says 『……唉！』 and the game goes on from there.

Verified on the emulator: the game starts from Play with nothing else to do, the title menu comes up, the intro and the story play, the Chinese text is all there, the character walks on the map and the conversations start. The field map and the battle interface were then taken apart as well — the next section writes down what is on the screen, what each key does, and how a battle is fought from the first move to the experience points.

The field map, the command ring and a landed attack

The field map is a tile grid that the camera follows. The arrow keys move the cursor one tile at a time, and the little panel in the bottom-right corner always describes the tile the cursor is standing on: a picture of the ground plus the attack and defence modifiers that the terrain gives. Plain ground reads A+05 D+00, and a forest reads A-05 D+10 — the forest's pair is exactly what the game's own rules give for 森林 (−5% AP, +10% DP).

Stand the cursor on one of your own people and press Space or Enter, and the game opens that character's window: name, class, level, HP, MP, DX, MV, HIT, AP, EV, DP, and the equipment with the AP and DP it adds. 悠妮 is a 人類 法師 at LV.01 with EX·00, HP 028/028, MP 008/008, DX·001, MV·04, HIT·086, AP·011, EV·001 and DP·007; 亞雷斯 is a 人類 騎士 at LV.01 with EX·00, HP 048/048, MP 000/000, DX·002, MV·07, HIT·092, AP·026, EV·002 and DP·006, carrying a 刺矛 (+AP 020) and 布衣 (+DP 002); 蓋亞 is a 機械 機兵 at LV.01 with HP 050/050, MP 000/000, DX·002, MV·04, HIT·092, AP·022, EV·002 and DP·014, carrying a 威力手臂 (+AP 015) and a 戰鬥裝甲 (+DP 008).

Around the selected character the game draws a ring of four command icons, and the layout is the one the game's own player notes describe: 上 physical attack, 左 spell attack, 右 items, 下 rest. The attack icons are drawn on a red plate and the other two on a blue one — the red plate is the command that is usable at that moment, and Space, Enter and Ins confirm it. The spell icon animates with sparks around it. Press Space on the character and the ring opens; press it again on a mage and the spell list comes up — 悠妮 knows one spell, 火炎術, priced at MP 02. Stand on empty ground, press Space, and a different ring opens instead: the field menu with 系統選單, 行軍, 設定 and 結束本回合, and Enter on it opens the system menu, which reads MAP·01 TURN·001, lists the victory condition 敵全滅 and the failure condition 索爾死亡, and counts the field as ENEMY·07 FRIEND·04 NPC·00 with ¥00000000 in the treasury.

F1 opens the global map — the whole field at a glance, with our side in blue and the enemy in red. The enemy on this map is a group of masked pirates, red headband over a dark visor, and their numbers are readable: put the cursor on one and the panel shows its HP (028) as it shows a character's.

Moving a character is two steps. Put the cursor on one of your own people and press Space: the game enters 進行移動, the character's stats appear in the panel at the top left, and the arrow keys now walk the cursor around while the tile panel follows it. Press Space again and the character walks to the tile the cursor was standing on. We moved 亞雷斯 three tiles west and two tiles south, from the path in the north to the grass next to a pirate.

Standing next to the enemy brings the command ring up around 亞雷斯, and now 物理攻擊 is the one on the red plate — the others turn blue. Space picks the target: the cursor jumps onto the pirate and its HP shows in the panel. One more Space lands the hit. The screen darkens into a night scene with a crescent moon and a sword comes slashing down, with 亞雷斯 LV·01 HP 048 MP 000 on one side and 盜賊 LV·02 on the other. The pirate went from 028 HP to 007 HP, and the game paid for the hit: 得到經驗31點！ — 31 experience points, shown on a panel with the character's portrait. Press Space on the enemy itself and the game opens its whole sheet: 盜賊, 人類 盜賊, LV·02, HP 007/028, EX 007/028, DX·002, MV·04, HIT·097, AP·024, EV·002, DP·004, carrying a 短劍 (+AP 010) and a 布衣 (+DP 002).

What is still open: the enemy turn. The 結束本回合 entry of the field menu is reachable — Ins on it pops 要結束本回合的行動嗎？ with YES and NO and 悠妮's portrait — but the answer could not be given from the app, so the enemies have not been seen to move or to strike back. The ring also only ever confirms its red entry: the direction keys moved the map cursor but never changed which icon the ring had picked, and neither did long key holds, Android key events, the numeric keypad scancodes, or taps and drags on the icons themselves. That is what the comments on this page are for.

The control scheme above is the game's own, not ours: the four commands around a character and the four entries of the field menu are laid out exactly as the game's player notes and the Chinese walkthroughs describe them — 人物處按確定：上面：物理攻擊，左面：法術攻擊，下面：休息，右面：物品裝備, and 空白處按確定：上=系統選單，左=行軍，右=設定，下=結束本回合.
