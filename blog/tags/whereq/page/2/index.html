「刚刚」​

"Just now"​

You come back to the game after lunch. You tap the slot that said you saved it five hours ago. The game loads exactly where you left it — the boss, the health bar, the room you were in.

The save is the save you made. The game is the game you left. Only the timestamp lies, and it lies in the direction that makes the app feel like it cannot be trusted with the one thing it is supposed to remember.

That is the whole of 1.0.0.13. It is the smallest release I have shipped and the only one where the fix is three lines long.

The guide I wanted and could not find​

Here is a thing I noticed while I was testing the app on my own phone. Every DOSBox guide I have ever read was written by somebody who already knew what a .zip bundle was. The music that stayed behind​

You freeze a game in the middle of a fight. The music is loud, the boss is doing something unpleasant, and you put the phone down to answer a message.

You come back two minutes later and load the state.

The fight is exactly where you left it — the sprites, the health bar, the position of the boss, all of it. That was 1.0.0.9. The snapshot was very good, and it was missing the sound.

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.12：存档搬出了应用

November 17, 2026 · 9 min read
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

「全没了」​

事情往往是这样发生的：手机存储满了，或者应用开始出怪问题，或者你只是想重装一遍。

你重新装上 APK，游戏压缩包还在你选的那个文件夹里，你按下 Play——游戏却从第一关开始了。

十个小时的进度没了。不是因为游戏丢了，而是因为应用没了。

在 1.0.0.12 之前，WhereQ-DOSBox 的情况就是这样。一个存档——整台机器、声音芯片、DOS 文件句柄——都躺在应用自己的私有目录里，而 Android 会在你卸载的那一刻把这个目录一起删掉。于是这个模拟器存在的意义——把一台跑着的 DOS 机器冻住、以后再接着跑——恰恰成了唯一保不住的那一样。

1.0.0.12 改了这件事。它还让读档之后靠鼠标操作的游戏不再卡死，也让模拟器读自己的版本号，而不是一个写死的旧数字。三件事，第一件最值得往下看。

WhereQ-DOSBox 1.0.0.12: your saves move out of the app

November 17, 2026 · 9 min read
Dazhi Zhang (Tony)

"Everything is gone"​

It happens the boring way. The phone is full, or the app starts misbehaving, or you simply decide to reinstall it. You install the APK again, the game bundle is still in the folder you picked, you press Play — and the game starts at the very beginning.

Ten hours of progress, gone. Not because the game was lost. Because the app was.

That was the truth about WhereQ-DOSBox until this release. A save state — the frozen machine, the sound chips, the DOS file handles — lived inside the app's own private storage, and Android deletes that storage the moment the app is uninstalled. So the one thing the emulator exists to protect was the one thing that could not survive the app.

1.0.0.12 changes that. It also makes a restore leave a mouse-driven game alive instead of hung, and it makes the emulator read its own version instead of a stale literal. Three things, and the first one is the reason to read the rest.
